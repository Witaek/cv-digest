## Top papers

- **You Don't Need Aligned Representations: Knowledge Distillation via Random Prototype Spaces (RPKD)** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37937), [code](https://github.com/HamzaEtcibasi/RPKD), [NeurIPS poster](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/poster/153784). Etcibasi, Cinbis. Feature distillation usually needs a projector to map student features onto the teacher's, which is hard when the architectures differ. RPKD skips that: teacher and student are each compared against the same bank of frozen random prototypes, and distillation happens in that shared prototype space rather than in either model's feature space. Reported gains: up to +7.98% over OFA (a heterogeneous-distillation baseline) on CIFAR-100, +12.44% on ImageNet-100, and +1.28% on ImageNet-1K. **Why it matters:** if your production model is a CNN or a mobile hybrid and your best teacher is a ViT or foundation encoder, this is a simple loss to try. Classification only in the abstract, so detection and segmentation transfer is untested.
- **GOFish: Full Field-of-View Gaussian Splatting and Mesh Extraction** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37818), [project](https://3dgofish.github.io/). Tu, Karumuri, Zhu et al. A 3D Gaussian Splatting method that trains directly on fisheye images, rasterizes beyond 180°, and extracts an aligned mesh, so you don't have to undistort and crop away the edge of the image. It reports +2.28 dB and +1.97 dB PSNR over the best prior method on the 200° FIORD and 205° FullCircle benchmarks, and renders in real time. Relevant if you reconstruct scenes from robot, drone or automotive fisheye rigs.
- **Can Event Detectors Leave the Broadcast Camera? Evidence from Tennis** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38042), [code](https://github.com/David-dsv/tennis-bounce-hit-benchmark). Soeiro-Vuong, Rochet. A 345-annotation benchmark of ball bounces and hits filmed from phones and court level. Supervised spotters that are near-perfect on broadcast video collapse here: E2E-Spot reaches a pooled bounce F1 of 0.130. Their RallyArb method keeps bounce F1 above 0.8, but on only three clips. **Why it matters:** a clear example of how badly event and temporal-action models break when the camera viewpoint changes. Budget for viewpoint-specific evaluation data.
- **Also in the Oct 30 listing (abstracts seen only as summaries, so no numbers):** SSQA, a linear-time quadtree attention for perception backbones compared against Mamba 2 ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38320)); ConvRoute-ViT, which keeps key tokens and compresses the rest into summary tokens to cut ViT compute ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38284)); and ST-Pruner, training-free visual-token pruning for VLMs that keeps most accuracy with about 11% of tokens ([link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38303), [code](https://github.com/sejong-rcv/ST-Pruner)).

## Today's deep read

**RPKD: Knowledge Distillation via Random Prototype Spaces** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37937), [code](https://github.com/HamzaEtcibasi/RPKD).

- **What to read:** the method section (how the random prototype bank is built, its size and dimensionality, and how teacher and student features are projected into it), then the heterogeneous pairs in the ImageNet-1K table, i.e. ViT→CNN and CNN→MLP pairs, against OFA.
- **Why it matters:** cross-architecture distillation is the usual way to get foundation-model quality into an edge model, and the projector between mismatched feature spaces is the fragile part. Frozen random prototypes remove the learned alignment step entirely.
- **How it could apply to you:**
  - **Try it on your classifier or backbone first:** distill a DINOv3/SigLIP-style teacher into the mobile backbone you deploy and compare against your current logit or feature KD.
  - **For detection, distill the backbone and neck:** the paper is classification-only, so apply the loss to pooled or per-level features and keep your detection losses unchanged. Measure mAP, not just top-1.
  - **Watch the gains on ImageNet-1K (+1.28%)** rather than the small-dataset numbers; that's the more realistic expectation at scale.
