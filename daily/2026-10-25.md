## TL;DR
- **supervision 0.31.2 changes Pascal VOC box conversion (breaking).** It now follows the devkit's 1-based inclusive convention, so box-only VOC files written by earlier versions load one pixel larger on the right and bottom. Re-export or re-check any VOC round-trip before upgrading.
- **Ultralytics 8.4.187 changes how YOLO26-pose training assigns anchors.** It matches on keypoint similarity (OKS) alone when keypoints are visible and adds an FCOS-style pyramid-level gate. It is on by default, so pose training results will shift. Inference and export are unchanged.
- **VANE pretrains a video encoder by predicting the next frame's embedding.** It beats V-JEPA 2.1 by +7.1 mean-class recall@5 on EPIC-KITCHENS-100 anticipation. CODI improves patch features in self-supervised ViTs by mixing a little of the teacher's class-token distribution into each patch target.

## Top papers

- **VANE: Learning from Next-Frame Prediction** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30724). From Yifeng Yao, …, Dongyan Zhao.
  - **The idea:** self-supervised video pretraining that predicts the *next frame's* representation in a frozen embedding space, instead of reconstructing masked tokens (MAE) or masked latents (V-JEPA). A lightweight auxiliary decoder keeps spatial detail so the encoder still serves dense tasks.
  - **Numbers:** +7.1 mean-class recall@5 over V-JEPA 2.1 on EPIC-KITCHENS-100 action anticipation. It claims SOTA among unified encoders on most anticipation, recognition and dense-perception benchmarks; the listing abstract gives no other values. No code is linked.
  - **Why it matters:** a direct challenger to V-JEPA 2.x as a video backbone. Check the code release before planning around it; none is linked yet.
- **CODI: Improving Self-Supervised Vision Transformers with Cross Distillation** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30639), [code](https://github.com/EldercatSAM/CODI). From Siran Dai, …, Qingming Huang (35 pages, 14 tables).
  - **The idea:** in DINO-style self-distillation, patch-token targets are noisy. CODI mixes a small amount of the teacher's class-token distribution into each teacher patch-token target, so patch targets inherit the more reliable image-level signal.
  - **Numbers:** improves both dense and image-level results under aligned pretraining; the listing abstract gives no values, so check the tables before planning a re-pretrain.
  - **Why it matters:** it is a change to the target, not the architecture, so it can drop into an existing DINO/iBOT recipe if you pretrain your own backbone on domain data.
- **WovenAnchor Matcher: Specialized Intra- and Inter-Image Context Modeling for Feature Matching** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30799), [code](https://github.com/mmaiLab/WovenAnchorMatcher). From Zhiyang Li, …, Nakamasa Inoue.
  - **How it works:** a semi-dense matcher (LoFTR/JamMa family) that splits the two jobs: a Mamba-based operator (WovenMamba) for context within each image and AnchorCrossAttention for context across the pair.
  - **Numbers:** 66.1 pose AUC@5° on MegaDepth, +2.0 over JamMa. The abstract gives no runtime figures.
  - **Why it matters:** a +2.0 AUC@5° gain over a recent Mamba-based matcher, with code, for SfM, visual localization or stitching pipelines that already use semi-dense matching.
- **EgoHand: Monocular 3D Hand Reconstruction from Egocentric Video** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30293), [weights](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/EgoHand-v1). 15 authors listed alphabetically; project leads Chao-Yeh Chen, Tiffany Chen and Umar Iqbal.
  - **The model:** trained on a 14M-image corpus with the SOMA-Hand parametric model. The `nvidia/EgoHand-v1` card describes an 883.5M-parameter model: a DINOv3 backbone initialized from SAM 3D Body, a transformer decoder with pose, shape and camera queries, and a 256×256 hand crop plus camera intrinsics as input.
  - **Numbers:** about 9.69 ms latency per the abstract (hardware not given in the listing), with zero-shot results on HOT3D, ARCTIC and H2O. The model card lists the HOT3D/H2O scores as "Undisclosed".
  - **License caveat:** the abstract says OpenMDW, but the HF card is gated under the NVIDIA Software and Model Evaluation License and says it is "not for production usage". Check which applies before building on it.

## Models & releases

- **Ultralytics 8.4.187** — [post](https://community.ultralytics.com/t/new-release-ultralytics-v8-4-187/2276), [release](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/releases/tag/v8.4.187), PR [#26843](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics/pull/26843). Released Oct 24.
  - **YOLO26-pose assignment:** for objects with visible keypoints, the assigner uses OKS only instead of blending OKS with box IoU, so anchors with better keypoints win.
  - **Scale gate:** an FCOS-style gate limits candidates to pyramid levels that suit each object's size; the finest and coarsest levels stay open for out-of-range objects, and objects that would get no candidates fall back to the ungated set.
  - No accuracy or speed numbers are given. Expect pose mAP to move when you retrain, and compare against a run on 8.4.186 if you track regressions.

## Edge & deployment

- **Evaluation of Front-End Feature Matching for Lunar Visual SLAM** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30774). Benchmarks 11 detector-matcher pairs on synthetic, lunar-testbed and terrestrial-analogue data. SuperPoint pairings beat SIFT with ratio-tested FLANN on the lunar testbeds (rotation-only metric), and SuperPoint + LightGlue is the fastest learned pair at 16 ms per frame. A useful reference point if you are choosing a front end for low-texture, harsh-lighting scenes.
- **SmolVLA-MoE** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30732), [code](https://github.com/dudu435/smolvla-moe). Replaces SmolVLA's dense action-expert FFNs with sparse MoE plus a shared expert: 84.25% average success on LIBERO (SmolVLA 82.75%, OpenVLA-7B 76.5%) with about 500M active of ~800M stored parameters.

## Today's deep read

**CODI** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30639), [code](https://github.com/EldercatSAM/CODI).

- **What to read:**
  - **The target mixing:** exactly how the class-token distribution is mixed into patch targets (a fixed coefficient or a schedule, before or after centering and sharpening). That one line is the whole method.
  - **Dense results:** linear-probe segmentation (ADE20K/VOC) and any detection fine-tuning numbers, and whether gains hold at ViT-B/L or only at ViT-S.
  - **"Aligned pretraining":** what the comparison controls (epochs, crops, register tokens) and whether baselines include DINOv2-style iBOT patch losses or KoLeo. Small SSL gains often vanish when recipes are matched.
- **How it could apply to you:**
  - **Domain pretraining:** if you continue DINO-style pretraining on your own unlabeled images (industrial, aerial, medical) before fine-tuning a detector or segmenter, this is a cheap change to the loss to try in an ablation.
  - **Measure on dense tasks:** evaluate with a frozen-backbone linear segmentation probe on your data, since patch-feature quality is what the method targets and what your downstream heads consume.
