## Today's deep read

**Roboflow Inference #3282 and #3290: readiness and model pinning** — [release](https://github.com/roboflow/inference/releases/tag/v1.8.4).

- **What to read:** the two PRs linked from the release. Look at what the readiness endpoint now waits for (shared weights packages plus every pinned model), and at how a preload failure is handled: the model stays pinned, so the server retries it instead of quietly serving without it.
- **Why it matters:** a server that reports ready before its weights are loaded gets traffic it can't serve. In practice that means the first requests after a rollout or autoscale time out or hit a cold load of a multi-GB model (SAM 3, a large detector). That shows up as p99 latency spikes that are hard to trace.
- **How it could apply to you:**
  - **If you run Inference on Kubernetes,** point the readiness probe at the server's readiness endpoint and set `initialDelaySeconds` / `failureThreshold` for your slowest pinned model's load time. Pods will now stay out of the Service longer, but they won't take traffic early.
  - **Watch your metrics after upgrading:** unloaded models disappear from `/metrics` (#3287), so dashboards or alerts keyed on per-model series may go quiet for models that were never loaded.
  - **Same pattern for your own servers:** whatever you serve with (Triton, TorchServe, a FastAPI wrapper), readiness should mean that warm-up inference has finished on every model you route to, not just that the process is up.
