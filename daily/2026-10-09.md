## Top papers

- **RT-DETR-World: Transferring Rich LLM Semantics to Real-Time Open-Vocabulary Detection** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09502). Per its title, it brings the RT-DETR real-time detector to open-vocabulary detection by transferring semantics from an LLM. Check the abstract for LVIS / COCO zero-shot AP and FPS against YOLO-World and other real-time open-vocabulary detectors.
- **UltraLight Luma: A Novel Edge-Deployable Perception Network for Crop-Row Segmentation in Agricultural Robotics** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11764). A very small segmentation network built to run on the embedded hardware of field robots. The target task is narrow, but the design choices may carry over to other thin-structure segmentation (lanes, cables, rails).
- **A Probabilistic Perspective on Wasserstein-Based Evidential Uncertainty for Out-of-Distribution Segmentation** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10116). An evidential-uncertainty method for flagging out-of-distribution pixels in semantic segmentation, framed with Wasserstein distances. Relevant if you need anomaly or unknown-object masks from a segmentation model in production (driving, inspection).
- **MultiFly: A Real-World Multimodal Aerial Dataset with Annotation-Efficient Label Transfer and Cross-Modal Semantic Consistency** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10359). A new multimodal aerial dataset whose labels are transferred across modalities to cut annotation cost. Useful as a benchmark if you work on drone perception with more than RGB.
- **Insights from Autoresearch for Solar Panel Segmentation** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10491). Reports what an automated (agent-driven) research loop found when tuning a solar-panel segmentation pipeline. Worth skimming for whether agent-run experiment search found anything a human sweep would miss.

## Today's deep read

**RT-DETR-World** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09502).

- **What to read:** how the LLM semantics are injected (offline text embeddings cached per vocabulary vs. anything at runtime), the zero-shot AP on LVIS and COCO, and the latency on a named GPU compared with YOLO-World and the closed-set RT-DETR baseline.
- **How it could apply to you:** if you deploy an open-vocabulary detector with a fixed customer vocabulary, the class embeddings can be precomputed, so the runtime cost is the detector alone. Check whether the paper exports to ONNX/TensorRT and whether it keeps RT-DETR's NMS-free decoding, which is what makes the latency predictable.
