## Top papers

- **Where Do Vision Language Models Fail at 3D Reasoning? (BedBench)** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39639), [code](https://github.com/leduckhai/BedBench), [project](https://leduckhai.github.io/BedBench/). Le-Duc et al. (incl. Savarese, Diab), Oct 29. BedBench has 13 procedurally generated tasks about 3D objects and tests 16 VLMs (12 open-weight, 4 frontier). The paper's point is that models can answer single questions correctly yet fail to recover an object's exact properties and use them consistently. On per-color counting, humans score 93.3% and the best model 7.2%. Fine-tuning raised one model from 3.0% to 68.6%, but its "most common color" answers still contradicted its own counts. A probe on Qwen3.5-27B found the exact counts recoverable 65.0% of the time, against 3.0% in its answers. Reasoning, scale and extra visual cues helped single judgments but never made exact answers reliable. **Why it matters:** if you use a VLM as a counter or inspector, measure exact correctness and whether its answers agree with each other, not just accuracy per question. Some apparent successes needed no image at all.

## GPU / CUDA / PyTorch

- **FLAMIA: FLAsh MIcroscaling Attention for fast and causally safe training on Blackwell** — [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03344), [code](https://github.com/yandex-research/flamia-blackwell-attention). Velikonivtsev, Koltakov, Kuznedelev, Nov 2. A family of attention kernels with an MXFP8 path and a mixed NVFP4/FP8 path. Low-precision attention normally has three problems, and FLAMIA addresses each:
  - **Quantization overhead:** quantized operands are reused across matrix orientations, and quantization is fused into the kernels.
  - **Causal leakage:** when later tokens share a scale factor with earlier ones, information can leak backward; FLAMIA adds safeguards against this.
  - **Forward/backward mismatch:** both passes use the same computation.

  Document-local scaling lets packed variable-length batches run without repacking. Forward plus backward attention, quantization included, is 1.10–1.40x faster on B200 and 1.06–1.42x on B300 than the fastest baseline for each workload. The repo benchmarks against FlashAttention-4, cuDNN, FlashInfer and SDPA. Llama-style pretraining was stable with quality preserved, though the abstract gives no accuracy figures. The repo needs SM100 (B200/B300), CUDA ≥ 13.0 and `nvidia-cutlass-dsl[cu13]==4.8.0`, and its API is `flash_attn_fp8(q, k, v, causal=...)`. This matters if you pretrain ViT or video backbones on Blackwell, especially with packed variable-resolution (NaFlex-style) sequences.

## Today's deep read

**YOLO27 docs, architecture section** — [docs](https://docs.ultralytics.com/models/yolo27).

- **What to read:** the two-family design. n and s are CNNs that keep NMS by default (`nms=False` switches to the one-to-one head) and drop the middle detection scale. m and l use an UltraViT backbone with attention in the deepest stage and a DETR-style query decoder with no NMS. Then read the FLOPs footnote: decoder attention is included, and the counts are for fused models.
- **Why it matters:** this is the first time the Ultralytics line splits by size into a CNN family and a DETR-style family. Things like INT8 behavior, how export handles ops, and latency scaling with resolution may differ a lot between s and m. For n and s, dropping the P4 scale could hurt mid-sized objects even as the enlarged early stage helps small ones.
- **How it could apply to you:**
  - **Plan the export test now.** If you deploy m or l, check that your runtime (TensorRT, OpenVINO, ONNX Runtime, NPU toolchain) handles the decoder's attention ops in INT8 before the weights land. DETR-style decoders are often where quantization fails.
  - **Pick the head for n and s deliberately.** The NMS-free head now costs 0.4 mAP. On edge targets where NMS is a CPU bottleneck, `nms=False` may now be worth it.
  - **Re-baseline sizes.** A 3.0M-param n at 42.6 mAP versus the YOLO26 entry (40.9 on the product page) could let you move down a size. Wait for the released checkpoints and run your own dataset before switching.
  - **Check the license at launch.** The page says YOLO27 licensing will be confirmed at release.
