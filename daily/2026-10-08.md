## Top papers

- **TR-PTQ: High-Accuracy Integer-Only Transformer Post Training Quantization via Taylor Region Reformulation** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09969). A post-training quantization method for transformers that keeps inference integer-only, based on a "Taylor region" reformulation (presumably of the non-linear ops). Integer-only matters for NPUs and DSPs that cannot run float softmax, GELU or LayerNorm. Check the abstract for which models and bit widths it reports.
- **DisParQ: Self-Supervised Part Concepts for Interpretable Vision Foundation Models** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09802). Learns part-level concepts with self-supervision on top of vision foundation models, so that their features can be interpreted. Worth a look if you debug DINO-style features in production.
- **InstanceBench: Diagnosing Referential Reasoning and Target Identity in Referring Expression Segmentation** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09478). A benchmark that tests whether referring-segmentation models find the right *instance* or only the right category. It is relevant if you evaluate grounding or promptable segmentation models.
- **Concentration, Not Uncertainty: Why Targeted Synthetic Data Doesn't Help Camouflaged Object Detection** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09807). A negative result on using targeted synthetic data for a hard detection setting. It is a useful check before you invest in synthetic data for rare or hard classes.
- **ActiveLang: Active Open-Vocabulary 3D Mapping with Semantic-Uncertainty-Guided Exploration** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09518). Open-vocabulary 3D mapping in which the robot chooses where to look next based on semantic uncertainty.

## Today's deep read

**TR-PTQ** — [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09969).

- **What to read:** how it approximates softmax, GELU and LayerNorm with integer arithmetic, and the accuracy gap to the FP32 baseline on ImageNet and on any detection or segmentation results.
- **How it could apply to you:** if you deploy ViT or DETR-style models on an NPU, list which ops in your exported graph currently fall back to float. Then check whether this method would remove those fallbacks, and what accuracy drop the paper reports at your target bit width.
